# Code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to improve the instrument.

Participants must:

- address evidence, mechanisms, and claims rather than people;
- preserve mixed results and local uncertainty;
- disclose conflicts, rights limits, and material benchmark assumptions;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and personal attacks;
- respect private challenge packs, embargoes, and vendor disclosure boundaries;
- correct the public record when evidence changes.

Maintainers may remove participation that makes rigorous collaboration unsafe or impossible.
